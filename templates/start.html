What OOTK does best

Its strengths are objective analysis and clear conceptual models.

  • Objective analysis. Each card's dignities, aspects and correspondences come from the Book T and Liber 777 tables and are scored the same way every time, so the same seed always gives the same figures.
  • Clear conceptual models. The heap, the wheel of houses and signs, and the Cube of Space show where each card sits and how the cards link.

The meaning is left to your AI.

Where the cards come from

Tarot began as a card game in 1440s Italy. Its occult meanings came much later, from the Golden Dawn in 1880s London, whose former members made both decks used here: A. E. Waite and Pamela Colman Smith in 1909, and Aleister Crowley and Lady Frieda Harris between 1938 and 1943.
