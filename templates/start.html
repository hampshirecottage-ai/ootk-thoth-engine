Your first reading, step by step

  1. Shape your question

    On the reading form, open Help me shape my question under the Question box. It asks which part of life the question touches, how you’d put it in your own words, what the reading should do (this picks the spread), whose question it is and over what span, and anything your AI should know. Then press Use this question. A question is optional: you can leave it blank.

    Shape a question

  2. Draw the cards

    Your question rides along with the cards but doesn’t change which cards come up. For a first try, three cards (past, present and future) or today’s card, which is the same for everyone, are easy places to start.

    Draw three cards Today’s card{% if sample %} See the sample{% endif %}

  3. Save the prompt as Markdown

    On the report, press Save prompt as Markdown (.md). The one file holds the cards, every calculation and your question.

  4. Attach it in your AI

    In Claude, ChatGPT or another AI, press its + button and add the file. Your AI writes the interpretation from the cards and calculations in the prompt.

To learn what the numbers mean, the Library explains each term in the report, and How it works shows how it is all worked out.

Reading a report yourself

Your AI writes the full interpretation, but you can read the main lines of any report yourself in three steps. Each step uses the sample above.

  1. Look at the element balance

    The leading element sets the tone: Fire for will and drive, Water for feeling, Air for thought, Earth for practical and material things. An element that is missing matters too: the cards say nothing through it.

    In the sample: {{ w.lead | join(' and ') }} {{ 'lead' if w.lead | length > 1 else 'leads' }} with {{ w.lead_count }} of {{ w.total }} cards{% if w.absent %}, and {{ w.absent | join(' and ') }} {{ 'are' if w.absent | length > 1 else 'is' }} missing{% endif %}.

  2. Find the weakened cards

    A card is ill-dignified when the elemental dignities with its neighbours add up to less than zero. In Book T a weakened card speaks with less force, or from its harder side; a well-dignified card speaks at full strength.

Where the cards come from

Tarot began as a card game in 1440s Italy. Its occult meanings came much later, from the Golden Dawn in 1880s London, whose former members made both decks used here: A. E. Waite and Pamela Colman Smith in 1909, and Aleister Crowley and Lady Frieda Harris between 1938 and 1943.
