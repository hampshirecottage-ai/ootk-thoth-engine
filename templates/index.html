LLM Tarot Reading

A neutral instruction set for an LLM to interpret a tarot reading. OOTK draws a Thoth spread and works out its dignities, aspects and Liber 777 correspondences, then writes them up as a prompt you paste into an LLM. It does not interpret the cards itself.
