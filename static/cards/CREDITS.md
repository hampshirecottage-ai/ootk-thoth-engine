# Card art credits

The images in this folder are Pamela Colman Smith's illustrations for the Rider-Waite-Smith
tarot (Rider & Company, London, 1909), designed with Arthur Edward Waite. They are in the
public domain: published in 1909, and Smith died in 1951, so the art is out of copyright in
both the United States and the United Kingdom. Only the 1909 printings are used; later
recoloured editions (such as the 1971 U.S. Games colouring) are copyrighted and are not.

Source scans: restored scans of a 1909 "Pam-A" printing, taken from
[mixvlad/TarotCards](https://github.com/mixvlad/TarotCards/tree/840b84d012c6b74dc1634fa2d00d1b21f5b28093/tarot/rider-waite)
at a pinned commit. `scripts/download_images.py` fetches them, cuts away each card's numeral
strip and title banner, and `scripts/optimize_images.py` builds these WebP copies.

The cards are shown under their Thoth titles, using the Golden Dawn correspondences both
decks share:

| Thoth | Rider-Waite-Smith |
|-------|-------------------|
| VIII Adjustment | XI Justice |
| XI Lust | VIII Strength |
| XIV Art | XIV Temperance |
| XX The Aeon | XX Judgement |
| XXI The Universe | XXI The World |
| Knight | King |
| Queen | Queen |
| Prince | Knight |
| Princess | Page |
| Disks | Pentacles |

The Thoth paintings by Lady Frieda Harris are still under copyright and are not included.
